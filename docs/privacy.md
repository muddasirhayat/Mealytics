# Mealytics Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Mealytics is a meal and water logger published by Codixr (`support@codixr.com`). This policy describes the Android app distributed as `com.codixr.mealytics`.

Mealytics is for people 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and the app does not let you set an age below 13.

## What stays on your phone

The app stores this information on the device only:

- The name you enter, your age, gender, height, weight, activity level, and goal
- The calorie and macro targets calculated from those answers
- Meals you log, custom foods, water intake, and an in-progress meal draft
- Recent food searches, theme (system, light, or dark), and whether sounds and haptics are on

That information is kept in the app’s local storage. It is not encrypted by Mealytics beyond what the phone already does. The Android app turns off Android Auto Backup for this app, so Mealytics does not ask Android to copy that storage into your Google account.

There is no Mealytics account, no sign-in, and no cloud copy of your diary that we operate.

## What leaves your phone

Food search looks at a catalog stored in the app first. That catalog is built from USDA FoodData Central (SR Legacy) and is already on the device.

If that catalog has no match, the app sends the search words to Open Food Facts (`search.openfoodfacts.org` and `world.openfoodfacts.org`) over HTTPS, so it can show branded products. Open Food Facts receives the query and standard request data such as an app user agent. Mealytics does not send your name, age, weight, or meal log with that request.

The Play build does not include a USDA API key, so it does not send searches to the live USDA API.

The app does not use advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services. It does not ask for your location, contacts, photos, or microphone.

## How targets are calculated

Calorie and macro numbers are estimates for logging. They are not medical advice, a diagnosis, or a diet prescription. Daily targets are kept at or above 1,600 kcal under age 18 and 1,200 kcal from age 18.

## Deleting your data

In the app, open Profile and choose **Delete all my data**. That removes the Mealytics data listed above from the device and returns you to the first-run screens. Uninstalling the app also removes it. There is no online account for us to delete.

## Open Food Facts and USDA

Nutrition facts for branded foods come from Open Food Facts, which is shared under the Open Database License (ODbL). The bundled generic-food catalog comes from USDA FoodData Central. Those projects have their own practices for data you send to their servers. Open Food Facts’ privacy information is on their site.
